How to evaluate the product

How Derma Honey Works: What to Know

A four-step framework for reviewing intended use, formula information, directions, evidence, and individual variation without making unsupported claims.

01

Identify the intended use

Start with the current official product description. Identify what the product is intended for before interpreting individual ingredients or reviews.

02

Review the formula

Look at the complete ingredient information and any available quantities, directions, warnings, and other label details.

03

Match evidence to the claim

Research about an individual ingredient is not automatically evidence that the finished product produces the same result. Keep those levels of evidence separate.

04

Consider individual variation

Personal experiences can differ. Routine, skin type, sensitivities, other products, and many individual factors can influence outcomes.

Keep the evidence in context

Intended mechanism is not the same as guaranteed outcome

A useful review distinguishes between what a product is intended to do, what its ingredients are known or researched to do, what evidence exists for the finished product, and what individual customers report.
